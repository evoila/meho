## Note
MEHO has four default roles: Reader, Operator, Admin and Platform Admin. MEHO now reads the old group MEHO-SA-Operators as Operators and the old group MEHO-Approvers as Readers. Nothing changes in Keycloak. These rights are lost on the upgrade: (1) a person in MEHO-SA-Operators does not approve their own change, except their own token `caution` change with a click in the web console; (2) a person in MEHO-SA-Operators does not approve another person's `dangerous` or `destructive` change, so their own waiting high-risk change needs an Admin; (3) a person in MEHO-Approvers approves nothing and does not see the Approvals pages; (4) MEHO does not read the `approver` claim of the token in any token form, so a login whose only approval right came from that claim reads like a Reader; (5) people in MEHO-Approvers get no "a change waits for you" push message, and people in MEHO-SA-Operators get it only for a change they may approve as an Operator. The upgrade sets the stored approval flags of every push subscription to false; the next sign-in stores the person's current flags. With approver routing on (`POLICY_APPROVER_ROUTING`), a routing entry in a policy file or on a waiting request that names an old kind (`SA-Operators` or `Approvers`) matches only the people whose token names that old group, never every Operator or Reader; `Approvers` matches nobody who may approve. A built-in `approver` login keeps working, as an Operator. Waiting requests stay, and the new rules apply to them at once.

## Action
Before you upgrade, put each person who must keep approving risky changes, or their own changes, into MEHO-Admins (or the Admins group of the tenant) and take them out of the old group. A person is in one role group per tenant, or the login is refused. Approve or reject waiting requests that only such a person could approve. Check your Helm values for overrides of `JWT_SELF_APPROVER_GROUP` and `JWT_ROLE_GROUPS`. No schema action: the data step runs with the other migrations.
