## Note
MEHO has four default roles: Reader, Operator, Admin and Platform Admin. MEHO now reads the old group MEHO-SA-Operators as Operators and the old group MEHO-Approvers as Readers. Nothing changes in Keycloak. People in MEHO-SA-Operators have the rights of an Operator: they approve another person's `safe` and `caution` change, and their own token `caution` change with a click in the web console. Only an Admin approves their own other changes, and another person's `dangerous` or `destructive` change. People in MEHO-Approvers have the rights of a Reader: they read, and they do not see the Approvals pages. People in MEHO-Approvers get no "a change waits for you" push message. People in MEHO-SA-Operators get it only for a change that they may approve as an Operator. The upgrade sets the stored approval flags of every push subscription to false; the next sign-in stores the person's current flags. A built-in `approver` login keeps working, as an Operator. Waiting requests stay, and the new rules apply to them at once.

## Action
Before you upgrade, put each person who must keep approving risky changes, or their own changes, into MEHO-Admins (or the Admins group of the tenant) and take them out of the old group. A person is in one role group per tenant, or the login is refused. Approve or reject waiting requests that only such a person could approve. Check your Helm values for overrides of `JWT_SELF_APPROVER_GROUP` and `JWT_ROLE_GROUPS`. No schema action: the data step runs with the other migrations.
