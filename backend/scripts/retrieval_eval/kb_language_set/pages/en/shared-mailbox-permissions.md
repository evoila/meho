# Shared mailbox: rights for a team

## What it is

A shared mailbox is one mailbox that a whole team reads and answers, for example the address of the service desk. It has no password of its own.

## Give a person access

1. Open the mailbox in the admin view.
2. Add the person under "Members".
3. Choose "Read" or "Full access". Full access lets the person send mail in the name of the team.

## Size

A shared mailbox has its own limit of 10 GB. It is not part of the limit of any member. The team lead decides who clears old mail.

## Remove a person

Remove the person from "Members". The change takes up to 30 minutes.
