## Note
In MEHO's own login realm, in `master` and in the target's admin realm, only a platform admin approves a change of rights. The note on a waiting change of rights and the `meho doctor` line "second admin" now count the platform admins on their own. The note shows when MEHO finds no platform admin in the tenant other than the person who asked, also when the tenant has other admins. Its last sentence is "MEHO found no other platform admin in this tenant." For a change that can change the approval sign-in, and for a change that sets the password of the asker's own login, the note leaves out the sentence "You can approve it yourself only if you are a platform admin and asked for it in the web console." `/api/v1/health` has a new field `approvals.rights_change_platform_admins` (the number of platform admins, or null when MEHO cannot count them). `meho_status` does not show it. `approvals.rights_change_admins` keeps its name and counts tenant admins and platform admins together. `meho doctor` warns "This tenant has no platform admin." or "This tenant has only one platform admin." and, for two or more, shows "N platform admins in this tenant". Who may approve does not change.

## Action
When the line "second admin" warns, add a platform admin other than the person who asks in Keycloak (in `MEHO-Platform-Admins`). A script that reads the text of the note or of the doctor warning reads the new text. No schema action.
